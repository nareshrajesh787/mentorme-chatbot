Our Impact
Serving Forsyth County youth for over 23 years, Mentor Me North Georgia has made a lasting impact in the community:
We have served over 5,000 children since 2002.
MM Connect Clubs has grown from 4 to 28 clubs in Forsyth County elementary, middle and high schools.
Founded in 2009, over 850 children have improved skills and bonded with mentors in our Mentoring After School program.
Our One-to-One mentoring program has matched over 600 mentors and mentees.
When possible, we add new programs and enrichment opportunities to offer even more opportunities for Forsyth County children to receive the positive support and benefits of mentoring.
$342,507 Raised in 2024
The generous support of individuals and businesses allows us to provide mentoring opportunities for young people.
23 Years of Serving Forsyth County
The goal is for all of our Mentor Me students to become healthy, strong and productive members of society.
820 Youth Served in 2024
Thanks to the dedication of our volunteers, we were able to connect 820 youth to positive role models and trusted adults.
