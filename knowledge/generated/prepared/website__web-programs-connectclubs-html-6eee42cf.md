Connect Club Program
Mentor Me North Georgia offers monthly Connect Club meetings at Forsyth County elementary, middle and high schools to encourage and empower students and connect them to peers and adult role models.
Connect Club provides information to equip these elementary, middle and high school participants in their everyday lives. We engage multiple volunteers to visit our Clubs as guest speakers and small group leaders who share life stories or teach life skills.
Connect Clubs are facilitated in partnership with Forsyth County Schools.
For more information on the Connect Club program, or to be a guest speaker at one of the meetings, please contact us at info@mentormenorthga.org or by calling (678) 341-8028.
Connect Club Student Refections:
90% Feel better equipped to manage emotions, stress, or difficult situations
85% Benefited from resources and club curriculum shared with students
70% Improved healthy relationship with peers and others in the community
Volunteer Requirements
Must be at least 21 years old.
Must commit to one or two 2-hour sessions per month.
